## 2. the forward-backward and side forces (Jaap's question)
MOTOR's ground solve (muscles.staged's first stage: balance_asked, two share_asked rounds, contact.FRICTION 0.5) on
the lab's kinematics, each carried foot a sole grid (heel to toe tip, 15 points, friction pyramids), the plates saying
only which foot is down. Both feet summed, MAE %BW, unfiltered 100 Hz (x forward, z right):
- walking: x 2.33, z 2.25 (plates' mean |x| 8.1, |z| 3.4; solved |x| 8.8); squats 1.06 / 0.57; sit-to-stand 2.87 /
  1.12; the pelvis' residual after it 1.1 %BW, 1.3 %BW*ht on walking. The solve carries the braking and push-off
  when the kinematics ask for them: no weight, cone or row in it forbids the horizontal force.
- The kinematics' own demand (the root's force rows, m a - m g, against the plates' sum): lab kin walking x 2.6,
  z 2.6; run kin walking x 17.4, z 5.6 %BW. The run's ground follows its demand (its residual's x is small); the
  anterior error is the video path's centre-of-mass acceleration, not the solve.
- Per foot the split is not seen: on the lab's kinematics each foot's x is off by 5.5-6.0 %BW on walking and z by
  4.5-5.8 on squats (plates 5.7 per foot, their sum 1.6), where the sum is right: forces that cancel between two
  feet on the ground put nothing on the pelvis' six rows, so the first stage cannot tell them. A design limit of
  solving the ground from the pelvis' balance (OpenCap's tracking simulation splits by its contact spheres), not a bug.
