MOTOR on OpenCap LabValidation: the subject's own standing pose as the model's neutral, 2026-11-08 (issue #98).
Idea: as a static calibration in motion capture, take each subject's static standing trial (static1, videos only) as
the model's zero for pelvis tilt, lumbar extension and the elbows, read from the subject's own video, never the lab's
angles. Chain: GEM-X (diagonal focal) -> MoGe-2 -> GEM-X at MoGe-2's field of view -> the sim body on the lab's floor
(the final validation's chain, sim body code unchanged since 5996a8f2), the 45 static videos (9 subjects x 5 cameras).
Neutral (`labval.neutral`): each static run's median over its frames; the pelvis tilt read on its own heading and
taken off as a turn about the pelvis' own z (`_run.angles`), lumbar extension and elbows less their standing values,
each trial on its own camera's static run. Scoring: `python -m tools.labval score <measure-final> --static
<measure-static>` against the same command without --static, the same 715 runs of the final validation.
Cost: GEM-X EUR 3.32, MoGe-2 EUR 1.36, GEM-X at MoGe-2's fov EUR 1.71, sim bodies EUR 1.01: EUR 7.40 (override
labval-motor-static, EUR 10). Results in the store: labval-results/motor-static-neutral-2026-11-08/.

Result: worse on every measure it targets, so not used (the validation's numbers stay those without --static).
The sim body stands at a pelvis tilt of -2.6 deg (median of 45 static runs, range -7.8 to +0.8), lumbar +1.0, elbows
7.5 deg flexed (0 to 23); the trials' biases against the lab run the other way (pelvis tilt +5.4, lumbar -3.7, elbows -23
and -25), so the lab's IK does not put a subject's standing pose at the model's zero: its scaled model, registered on
the subject's static markers, stands at its own tilt and elbow flexion. Zeroing the standing pose adds the sim body's
own standing reading to each bias.
