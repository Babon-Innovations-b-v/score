PBL (the Portable Biomechanics Laboratory; Peiffer, Cotton et al. 2025, arXiv:2507.08268) on OpenCap LabValidation, single camera, scored with tools/labval's own scorer: MOTOR's baseline beside OpenCap (#98), 2026-11-08.
Code: IntelligentSensingAndRehabilitation/MonocularBiomechanics at 118de08f (AGPL-3.0), fetched pinned and installed from its own uv.lock (JAX 0.10.1 CUDA 12, MuJoCo MJX 3.9.0, TensorFlow 2.21.0; opencv-python swapped for opencv-python-headless 4.13.0.92), MeTRAbs metrabs_eff2l_y4_384px_800k_28ds (its load_metrabs model, bml_movi_87; models non-commercial: a research baseline), model humanoid_torque.xml (LocoMuJoCo, MIT). SCORE side: tools/labval/pbl (pbl_env.sh, on_pbl.py, cluster.py, score.py).
Pipeline as released (main.py): MeTRAbs on every frame of the 60 fps video at its defaults, then PBL's static-camera fit (fit_model, lr 1e-3, 10,000 steps, its defaults), the trajectory read at every frame. Given: each video's camera as the chain's own (GEM-X's K at MoGe-2's field of view; the demo's default is a Samsung S20's), the largest MeTRAbs detection a frame, confidence 0 on a frame with none. Never the lab's calibration.
Pelvis: PBL's static fit has no gravity (its world is the camera), so its pelvis turn is levelled on the floor MoGe-2 sees in that video (the chain's own floor, tools/labval/_floor.seen), its heading put on the lab's as the chain's is; "camera up" (the camera's -y as up) beside it.
Run: 720 single-camera inputs (144 non-static trials x 5 cameras), 48 jobs of 15 videos on the cluster's L4 cards, 3 PBL processes side by side a card (a fit step takes 9 ms at 95 or 480 frames: one fit leaves the card mostly idle), up to 33 cards at once beside MOTOR's runs, 140 min, 1355 node minutes, EUR 17.83 (run pbl-labval-2); 2 of the 720 from the smoke run (pbl-smoke-3, EUR 0.12). Before it: pbl-smoke-1 (TensorFlow saw no card: libcusolver not on its path), pbl-smoke-2 (MuJoCo's EGL), pbl-labval-1 (a lane's later videos failed until main.py's clear_caches was done before each fit), stopped. Spend under override pbl-labval-20261108 (EUR 30): EUR 22.66 in all, EUR 4.66 of it the two stopped runs booked by hand from what was watched (their submitter was killed before it booked).
Scorer: tools/labval as on main (unchanged; tools/labval/pbl/score.py maps and calls it: the time shift on the legs' flexions, overlap 0.5, the per-subject bootstrap of 20,000 draws), the same labval.angle_scores and pooled_angles as the chain's labval-final-2026-11-07.txt, whose report.json is scored beside it here (its 5.16 / 3.06 reproduced).
Coverage: all 720 of 720 inputs ran and scored (none failed); 15 videos had frames MeTRAbs found no person on (80 frames of them in all, at most 33 of 803 on subject4_squatsAsym1_Cam2), which PBL's fit takes at confidence 0. The chain's report has 715 (its 5 failures): "trials both have" compares the two on the same trials.
mtp_angle: the lab's model welds the MTP (LaiArnoldModified2017_..._weldMTP), its IK holds about -5 deg throughout; PBL's MTP is free, so its 26 to 28 deg MAE is the two models' different foot, not a tracking error. Not in the matched 15.
OpenCap Monocular's 4.8 is its paper's own number on its own set, not rescored here.
