# env1: concept against scene, the shared causes

Input: 7 groups of per-place findings for all 26 env1 places (read-only). Scores: airlock 2, base 3, camp 2, campgrounds 4, cockpit 2, docking_airlock 1, engineroom 4, flat 3, galley 2, garage 1, greenhouse 1, habitat 1, hangar 2, hub 2, lab 3, old_station 3, quarters 2, shiplock 2, square 1, stairwell 1, storage 3, street 1, tube 3, walkway 3, workshop 3, wreck 2. **Mean 2.2/10, best 4 (campgrounds, engineroom).**

Most stills are the lit stage, with no settle and no people (base: OOM at 8 GB with 638 materials; square: OptiX/HIP 247). So people, crowds and settle positions are not judged here. That accounts for part of the gap in hangar, lab, old_station, tube, workshop, wreck and square, and for most of square.

Ranked by places hit times how badly. "Breaks" means the finding breaks the read in that place.

---

## 1. Shells: the wrong size, the wrong shape, or a ship plan that overrides the concept (18 places, breaks in 14)

**Cause.** The room's outline, height and shape come from a plan writing or a ship plan, not from the concept. On top of that, the shell builders cannot make several shapes the concepts draw.

There are three sub-causes:

- **1a. The plan's metres are wrong.**
  - airlock: re-fix at 0.00577 m a unit "by its rows' metres", giving 4.7 x 5.2 m against the locked 13.1 x 13.1 m.
  - hub: the floor box is 18 m while the walls say 9 m, and the record builds on the box.
  - workshop: 16 x 14 x 8 m asked, 11 x 11 x 4.2 m built.
  - square: 29 m against the game's 74 x 120 m.
  - docking_airlock: 3.1 x 3.0 m from Gemini metres.
  - cockpit: wall height taken from the inventory's hull_shell row; the plan's 2.43 m and its slope go unused.
  - Most of this is the plan writing's scale choice.
- **1b. The builders lack shapes.**
  - Dome with no rise (camp: eave = top = 8 m).
  - Round tube (tube: an arch whose rise equals its span, not a semicircle).
  - Wedge or sloped nose (cockpit).
  - Sunken pit (greenhouse, and hub's pit_radius 2.33 is never built).
  - Glass shells and roofs (camp, greenhouse, hub skylight: glass null/false).
  - Rectangle with truss beams (garage got "domed").
- **1c. Ship plan v7 overrides the concept.**
  - walkway: 1.5 m wide.
  - storage: door 0.9 x 2.0 m against the concept's tall hex double door.
  - shiplock: 1.95 m clear height.
  - quarters: its door and walkway opening.
  - lab: template doors.
  - **This is a decision, not a bug:** the game plan and the concept disagree, and code cannot pick which one wins.

**Places.** airlock, base, camp, cockpit, docking_airlock, engineroom (minor), garage, greenhouse, hub, lab, quarters, shiplock, square, storage, street (sawtooth outline across the road, 3.5 m facades), tube, walkway, workshop.

**Fix.**
- 1a: days. Check the plan's metres against the locked design plan and the inventory's room row, and refuse a re-fix that shrinks the room by more than about 30 %.
- 1b: days per shape (dome rise, round tube, sloped walls, pit, glass), several weeks in all.
- 1c: owner's decision first, then small.

**Moves closer?** Yes, the most of any cause. The shell is the frame of every interior still. With the wrong size and shape, no object fix makes it read.

## 2. Code rows bring no geometry: walls, frames, panels and terrain are blank (≈20 places, breaks in 8)

**Cause.** Inventory rows of kind "code" never become geometry: wall_panels, ceiling_panels, frames, ribs, padded walls, hazard frames, beams, facades, stairs, craters and terrain. The earth seed's kit reports "the design lays no kit piece". So every wall is one flat library finish.

**Places.** airlock, cockpit, docking_airlock, engineroom, flat (green dado), galley, garage (beams), greenhouse (stairs: 0 plan items), habitat (partitions as full-height slabs), hub, lab, quarters, shiplock, stairwell, storage, street (facades), walkway (padded sections), workshop, old_station and wreck (craters), campgrounds (cliffs and mesas: terrain {}).

**Fix.** Big. A wall-kit capability is needed: panel, frame, rib, dado and padded-section pieces drawn from score_lib and laid along the shell from the code rows, plus a crater and terrain layer. Every piece has to come from the library (hard rule), so library content has to be added as well as code.

**Moves closer?** Yes, a lot for interiors. The walls fill half of every frame, and "flat grey box" is the most common one-line verdict.

## 3. Model fitting makes things too small (17 places, breaks in 10)

**Cause.**
- `bake_job.fit_within` scales a model as one block to its tightest side, which for doors, lockers and panels is usually the thin depth.
- `fitting.in_opening` uses `scale=min(1.0, tall/size)` (fitting.py:801), so a model is never grown to fill its opening.
- Openings are cut at the reading's size, so the leaf ends up much smaller than its hole.

Some models were also made at the wrong size or proportions to begin with: the workshop monitors (0.16 m), the wreck's rear half, the cockpit windows (0.18 m).

**Places.**
- **Doors:** airlock, cockpit, docking_airlock (hatch 1.02 m), engineroom (1.55 m), hangar, lab (1.46 m in 2.66 m), quarters (1.55 m), shiplock (vault 0.65 m), stairwell (gate 1.25 m), storage.
- **Furniture:** quarters desk and lockers, lab desk (0.92 m against a 4.3 m plan box), hangar desks and stair, hub consoles, workshop benches and monitors, airlock suit and pipes, cockpit screens and console.
- **Other:** square (palms, lamps, buildings), wreck (rear half), base (module sizes).

**Fix.** Days, and mostly small code. Fit by the row's governing dimension (height for doors, lockers and people-scale things; footprint for desks) and allow non-uniform scaling within a limit. Let `in_opening` grow a leaf to its opening. Models that were made wrong need remakes, which may be paid.

**Moves closer?** Yes, clearly. Doors and hatches are focal points in about half the concepts.

## 4. The inventory reading drops key objects and makes duplicates (22 places, breaks in 7)

**Cause.** Two halves:

- **4a. Drops.**
  - The agreement rule (`reading.py` METHOD 5, ONLY_GEMINI near line 136) drops every row "only Gemini named it", and some rows "only the outline finder found it".
  - The match is by **noun**, not by box. In storage, SAM's "box" lies inside Gemini's crate_large_flat box and the row is still dropped.
- **4b. Duplicates.**
  - SAM returns several overlapping boxes on one object, which becomes count > 1: cockpit 4 doors, docking_airlock 2 airlock doors, flat 3 balcony doors and 2 stoves, shiplock 3 sliding doors, square 3 stage screens, walkway 2 ducts, wreck 5 solar panels (a hull panel merged in).
  - Each extra door copy then gets "an opening cut for it … the plan has no free door opening". That is where many of the extra **holes in the walls** come from: cockpit, docking_airlock, flat, shiplock, quarters, and probably stairwell.

**Places.**
- **Drops:** airlock (console, suit locker), camp (wardrobe, spacesuits), campgrounds (power station), cockpit, docking_airlock, engineroom, flat, hangar, hub, lab, quarters, shiplock, stairwell, storage (13 of about 15 crates), walkway, workshop, wreck.
- **Missing with no skip recorded:** habitat (ring light, bed, shower), tube (tool board).
- **Duplicates:** cockpit, docking_airlock, flat, shiplock, square, walkway, wreck, engineroom (count 4, 3 boxes), galley (cabinets 3 x 3.5 m).

**Fix.**
- 4a: small to days. Agree by box overlap, not by noun. Keep Gemini-only rows that fall on a SAM box under any noun.
- 4b: small. Merge overlapping SAM boxes before counting. Never cut a new door opening for a surplus door copy; drop the copy instead.
- New rows need new models, which is paid bake time.

**Moves closer?** Moderately. It brings back hero objects (airlock console, storage crates, the base's power station) and removes the extra doors and holes, which today break the read.

## 5. Placement rules ignore what things belong to (≈24 places, breaks in 15)

**Cause.** Layout is decided by generic rules, not by the relations the concept shows. There are four sub-causes:

- **5a. Nothing says what connects to what. The overlap fixer then pushes connected pieces apart** ("moved/parted X m out of").
  - base: tubes all north–south, none from the hub; greenhouse and hangar parted 6 and 14 m.
  - campgrounds: tunnel moved 7.9 m off the domes.
  - old_station: tunnels miss the hub.
  - workshop: tunnels stand across the doorways.
  - square: lantern strings end 24–29 m short.
  - street: lantern string at 24 m, lanterns at 6 m.
  - wreck: cables as 3.7 m slabs.
  - **Breaks the read in every one of these exteriors.**
- **5b. Copies of a row are lined up or spread.** The rule is "against the wall nearest its plan spot; packed along its walls", with one anchor per row, so everything that sits "on" something piles onto one holder.
  - galley: cabinets on 3 walls, 5 utensil racks.
  - garage: 10 monitors in a row, 14 boxes in one line.
  - quarters: all clutter on one desk.
  - workshop: monitor grid at knee height.
  - hangar: crates in rows.
  - habitat: lamps and curtains on the floor.
  - airlock: pipes.
- **5c. Fixed heights and facings.**
  - "Small wall thing centred 1.5 m up": docking_airlock jump seats.
  - Desk items at 0.8 m whatever the desk is: lab.
  - Code-row lamps at the record's spot y 0: quarters.
  - A null facing defaults to N, so a table ignores its box shape: galley (`holders/__init__.py` ~1980).
  - A turn to the facing swings the long axis across the plan: wreck halves side by side.
  - Pipes run flat at 10 m: hangar.
- **5d. The plan writing reads positions off an isometric picture.**
  - habitat: diagonal staircases of bunks and sofas.
  - camp: dome 2's lab furniture in dome 1.
  - cockpit: consoles and windows on the door wall.
  - tube: windows inside the room, pipes and cables on the floor centre.
  - street: AC units and a stair in the road.
  - walkway: doors moved off their keypads.

**Fix.**
- 5a: days to big. A "joins A to B" relation in the plan, and an assembly that builds connectors between their ends and exempts them from the overlap fixer.
- 5b and 5c: days. Per-copy anchors and holders, heights taken from the holder's top, facing taken from the plan box's long side.
- 5d: big. The plan writing has to un-project the cutaway picture (floor-plane homography from the SAM boxes) or check its positions against the concept. Some of this may never be exact from one picture.

**Moves closer?**
- 5a: yes, a lot, for the 7 exteriors and connected bases. It is their whole composition.
- 5b to 5d: noticeable everywhere, though each is a smaller step.

## 6. The film's first camera is not the concept's view (21 places, breaks in 12)

**Cause.** The film opens at a door or a standing spot, with a 1.6 m eye, fov 80, aimed across the room. There is no check that it frames the concept's composition, and some eyes stand inside or right against objects:
- habitat: inside bunk bed 1.
- wreck: inside the front rocket's box.
- shiplock: 0.25 m outside the south wall.
- garage, docking_airlock: shelves and a locker 0.7 m from the lens.

The concepts are mostly high cutaways or overviews: quarters, stairwell, workshop, old_station, wreck, square ("seen from the balcony"), walkway, camp. The latest commit (ae0f57ed6 camera_paths) touches the inside-a-solid test, which may already cover part of this.

**Places.**
- **Breaks:** airlock, docking_airlock, garage, habitat, hangar, old_station, quarters, shiplock, square, stairwell, storage, wreck.
- **Noticeable:** camp, flat, greenhouse, street, workshop.
- **Minor:** cockpit, galley, lab, walkway.

**Fix.** Small to days. Add a "concept view" camera fitted to the concept (a high cutaway: eye placed from the concept's vanishing lines or simply raised above the open wall). Add a near-clearance check of at least about 1.5 m and an aim at the room's densest side.

**Moves closer?** It changes the still's score a lot for little work. It **does not fix the scene**: on its own it shows the same problems more clearly. Do it so that reviews judge the scene and not a blocked lens.

## 7. Light: one brightness match, lamps in the wrong place, no mood (20 places, breaks in 4)

**Cause.** There are four parts:

- **Brightness only.** The light layer matches the concept's mean brightness by scaling lamps. In engineroom that is x45; storage ends 35 % over the concept. On pale walls this washes the room out, and contrast and colour are never matched.
- **Lamps in the wrong place.** Lamps sit at the record's spot, not on their laid model: quarters (on the floor), shiplock (inside the roof slab, so the room is lit red and blue), storage (door glow at the plan spot).
- **Emissives too faint.** Screens 0.03–0.05, cores and grow lights weak or missing.
- **Outside light where it should not be.** Daylight floods in through open roofs and openings (hangar: 127 klux sun through a 27 x 55 m roof hole; workshop), and the street is marked inside: true with no sky.

**Places.** Breaks: hangar, habitat, hub, quarters, shiplock. Noticeable to minor: airlock, camp, campgrounds, cockpit, docking_airlock, engineroom, flat, galley, greenhouse, old_station, stairwell, storage, street, workshop, wreck.

**Fix.** Days.
- Put lamps on their laid model.
- Match contrast and dominant colour as well as brightness.
- Set a floor for emissive energy.
- Take inside/outside from the plan.

**Moves closer?** Noticeably for mood. It is secondary to causes 1 to 5.

## 8. Finishes are off the concept (≈16 places, breaks in 3)

**Cause.** Two parts:
- Bakes come out far from their close-ups: dE 30–57, "bakes N % of its area over 20 a*b* off", orange rust or camouflage patterns where the concept has plain steel.
- "No library surface fits" is accepted as a gap.

**Places.** garage (white crates, breaks), greenhouse, habitat, galley, hangar, hub, lab, old_station, shiplock, tube, wreck, campgrounds, walkway (gunmetal token renders light, breaks), workshop (black desk), stairwell (one plaster over 11.8 m, breaks), flat.

**Fix.** Days for the bake's colour fault (rust patterns in particular look like one systematic cause). The "no surface fits" gaps need new score_lib surfaces, which is library content, not code.

**Moves closer?** Moderately.

## 9. Openings and the outside are empty (≈13 places, noticeable)

**Cause.** Two parts:
- "Sealed" doorways are cut and left open (hangar, lab), and doorways to tubes and neighbours show a void (greenhouse, habitat, hub, walkway, workshop, flat).
- Far ground, terrain and neighbours are not built or not composed into the still: campgrounds (cliffs), street (vista), old_station and wreck (craters), hub (tubes), flat (the square).

**Fix.** Small for sealed openings: do not cut them, or fill them with a leaf. Days for composing neighbours and far ground into a place's stage.

**Moves closer?** Noticeably for exteriors, less for interiors.

## 10. Ceiling height does not match the plan (5 places)

**Cause.** The scene record's ceiling and wall height differ from the plan's height:
- quarters: an 8 cm slit under the roof.
- shiplock: lights above the roof.
- docking_airlock: a 0.5 m gap.
- storage: 3.51 against 3.2 m.
- flat: a black strip.

**Fix.** Small: one source for the height.

**Moves closer?** Small, except in shiplock, where it is the light fault.

## 11. The likeness check passes bad code-made models (≈6 places)

**Cause.** The likeness check accepts code-made models it should reject:
- greenhouse: railing outline **0.0765** passes.
- galley: door slab at 0.80.
- campgrounds: comm mast as a lumpy column.
- stairwell: railing as a 3 m sausage, reused 12 times, running through the wall.
- quarters: string lights as rods.
- walkway: handrails tilted.

**Fix.** Small: a real outline floor for code-made models. Check fixes are mine, tested both ways.

**Moves closer?** Indirectly: it forces remakes.

## Place-specific problems with no shared cause

- **base:** the still OOMs (638 materials, 8 GB). No room is a place, so the hub, habitat and greenhouse are single picture-made shells.
- **square:** render failure (OptiX/HIP 247). assembly is blocked by likeness@people. The crowd is most of the concept.
- **habitat:** the still shows record positions while the accepted settle holds others ("drifts 1 cm" against metres apart). settle is accepted but no assembly takes it.
- **campgrounds:** habitat_dome_2 is in the record but absent from the still (unexplained). The lander ramp is a 40-step stair.
- **flat:** the balcony door model was made from the red-lit crop, so it is opaque pink.
- **stairwell:** settle failed twice (door_mat_1 tips 0.0799). Six south-wall openings exist with no plan door behind them (source not found).
- **wreck:** the engine nozzle is laid inside the rear hull, so there are two nozzles. The scorch decal and craters don't show (untraced).
- **hangar:** the ship stands about 1 m above the floor (from a pixel estimate). Its 8.45 m height is against the inventory's 4 m.
- **workshop:** the desk renders black though its texture is brown (untraced).
- **old_station:** a grey patch in the sky (untraced).
- **airlock:** the newer assembly (11-15) is not accepted.

## What code cannot fix

- **The game plan or the concept: one has to win.** Ship plan v7 sizes (walkway, storage, shiplock, quarters) and the square's 29 m against 74 x 120 m are a design choice for the owner.
- **Cutaway concepts and in-room film eyes cannot match.** A concept drawn as an isometric cutaway cannot be matched by an eye inside a closed room. Either the review compares from a cutaway camera with one wall hidden, or the score is capped.
- **Layout from one isometric picture is ambiguous.** Un-projecting helps (5d), but depth order and hidden objects will stay partly wrong. The camp dome mix-up and the habitat staircases are of this kind.
- **Library content.** Wall kits, new finishes and crater terrain must be added to score_lib before code can lay them.
- **Remakes.** Wrongly made models (wreck rear, workshop monitors, flat door, comm mast, railings) are remakes, which may be paid.
- **People and crowds** depend on the people layer and accepted assemblies (square, hangar, lab, tube, wreck, workshop, old_station).

## Verdict

The scenes are not close. The mean is 2.2/10, and no place reads as its concept. The individual models are often fair. What fails is the scaffolding:
- the shell is the wrong size and shape and its walls are bare (1, 2);
- things are shrunk to fit (3);
- key rows are dropped or doubled at reading (4);
- placement ignores what joins and holds what (5).

Fixing 3, 4, 6, 10 and 11 is days of mostly small code and would raise the still scores clearly, to a guess of about 3.5–4. Getting near the concepts also needs 1, 2 and 5a. Those are the big items: shell shapes, a wall kit from the library, and connectors. Two choices are the owner's first: whether the ship plan or the concept wins, and how a cutaway concept is reviewed against a film eye.
