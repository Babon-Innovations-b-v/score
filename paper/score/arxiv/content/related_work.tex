\section{Related work}\label{related-work}

OpenUSD began at Pixar in 2012 as the scene description for assembling its films \citep{openusd} and was released as open source in 2016 \citep{pixar2016usd}. A USD stage is composed from layers, references and variants, so that each tool edits its own layer. In 2023 Pixar, Adobe, Apple, Autodesk and NVIDIA founded the Alliance for OpenUSD to standardise it \citep{aousd2023}; its Core Specification 1.0, ratified in December 2025, covers composition and data representation and leaves physics to later domain specifications \citep{aousd2025corespec}. The UsdPhysics schema already describes rigid bodies, colliders, mass and joints \citep{usdphysics}, and Isaac Lab simulates such stages for robot learning \citep{mittal2025isaaclab}, but every object's mass, collider and joints must still be authored.

MuJoCo \citep{todorov2012mujoco}, built on generalised coordinates and soft contact, has been open source since 2022 \citep{mujocorepo} and is widely used in robotics and musculoskeletal simulation \citep{caggiano2022myosuite}. MuJoCo Warp ports it to the GPU \citep{mujocowarp, warp}, and Newton, an open engine under the Linux Foundation since September 2025, runs it as its main solver and reads OpenUSD directly \citep{newton}. MuscleMimic assembles MyoFullBody, a head-to-toe body with 416 muscles, from MyoSuite's models \citep{li2026musclemimic}. Each still needs a scene whose physics has been authored.

World generation models do not supply such scenes. Genie 3 generates interactive worlds as pixels from a closed model \citep{genie3} and Cosmos predicts video \citep{agarwal2025cosmos}; neither holds exportable geometry or physical state. HunyuanWorld 1.0 lifts panoramas into layered meshes with baked colour and no physical properties \citep{hunyuanworld2025}, and its successor reconstructs splats and meshes with coarse collision but no per-object mass or joints \citep{hyworld2}. Object generators such as TRELLIS.2 \citep{xiang2025trellis2} produce single assets with no scale, mass or relation to a scene, and procedural and language-driven systems keep scenes explicit but draw on a fixed grammar or asset set \citep{raistrick2023infinigen, raistrick2024indoors, yang2024holodeck}. What is missing is a framework that composes current commercial and open-source components into one route from a concept to a scene a simulator can step.
