\section{Related work}\label{related-work}

Film met the problem of assembling large scenes from many hands before simulation did. Pixar developed Universal Scene Description for its films and released it as open source in 2016 \citep{openusd, pixar2016usd}. A USD stage is composed from layers, so that each department edits its own layer without overwriting the others. In 2023 Pixar, Adobe, Apple, Autodesk and NVIDIA founded the Alliance for OpenUSD to standardise the format \citep{aousd2023}, and its Core Specification 1.0 was ratified in December 2025 \citep{aousd2025corespec}. The UsdPhysics schema adds rigid bodies, colliders, mass and joints \citep{usdphysics}, and Isaac Lab already trains robots on such stages \citep{mittal2025isaaclab}. The format can therefore hold a world that is ready for simulation, but every object's physics must still be authored by someone.

Physics engines have followed a similar path towards openness. MuJoCo \citep{todorov2012mujoco}, built on generalised coordinates and soft contact, became open source in 2022 \citep{mujocorepo} and is now a standard tool in robotics and musculoskeletal research \citep{caggiano2022myosuite}. MuJoCo Warp brings it to the GPU \citep{mujocowarp, warp}, and Newton, an open engine hosted by the Linux Foundation, uses it as its main solver and reads OpenUSD directly \citep{newton}. With MyoFullBody, a whole-body model with 416 muscles \citep{li2026musclemimic}, the same engine can carry people as well as objects. What these engines lack is not capability but scenes to run.

World generation models promise such scenes but deliver pictures of them. Genie 3 \citep{genie3} generates interactive worlds as pixels from a closed model, and Cosmos \citep{agarwal2025cosmos} predicts video; neither yields geometry that a simulator can load. HunyuanWorld \citep{hunyuanworld2025, hyworld2} lifts panoramas into meshes and splats, but with baked colour and without per-object mass or joints. Object generators such as TRELLIS.2 \citep{xiang2025trellis2} make convincing single assets with no scale and no place in a scene, while procedural systems \citep{raistrick2023infinigen, raistrick2024indoors, yang2024holodeck} keep their scenes explicit but are bound to a fixed grammar or asset set. What is missing is a framework that combines the strongest of them, commercial and open, into one route from a concept to a scene a simulator can step.
