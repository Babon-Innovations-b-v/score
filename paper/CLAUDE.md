# paper/CLAUDE.md

The papers, one folder each: `score/` (SCORE) and `motor/` (MOTOR, #98). **SCORE's one source is
`score/source/score.md`.** Everything under `score/out/` and `score/arxiv/` is generated by `score/build/build.py`;
never edit a `.tex` or the PDF by hand.

```
make paper                             # rebuild score/out/score.{tex,pdf} and score/arxiv/ (main.tex, content/, main.bbl, main.pdf)
python3 score/build/build.py --check   # exit 1 when a committed output is stale (also run by make check and the pre-commit hook)
(cd motor && tectonic -X compile main.tex)   # rebuild motor/main.pdf and main.bbl
```

## Layout

- `score/`: the SCORE paper.
  - `source/`: `score.md` (front matter: title, author, date, abstract), `notes.md` (background the paper does not print, the former appendix; the build skips it), `refs.bib`, `ieee.csl`, `markers.lua` (gap boxes and TODO marks).
  - `figures/` (when there are any): the pictures the draft uses, JPEG, ours and safe to publish.
  - `evidence/`: copies of the run records its numbers are read from (benchmark sessions, triage and resting summaries, logs), scrubbed of local paths and addresses.
  - `build/`: the build script and the check that the build is current.
  - `out/`: pandoc's standalone `.tex` and `.pdf` (citeproc, IEEE), and `stamp.json`, the hashes the check compares.
  - `arxiv/`: upload this folder as is. One `content/<section>.tex` per top-level heading, natbib with
    `unsrtnat`, and `main.bbl` because arXiv does not run BibTeX. `main.tex` compiles with pdflatex
    and with xelatex/tectonic.
- `motor/`: the MOTOR paper (#98), a second paper written directly in LaTeX: `main.tex`, `references.bib`,
  `figures/` (with `figures/teaser/`, the scripts that make Figure 1 again from a run folder: `prepare.py`, Blender's
  `render.py`, `compose.py`), the built `main.pdf` and `main.bbl` (`tectonic -X compile main.tex`), and `evidence/`, the sim-body run
  records its numbers are read from. Same class, margins and natbib style as SCORE's; gap boxes are `\gapbox`,
  numbers still moving are `\interim`. Its story is the one agreed with the owner (#98 comment 6723992362): SOMA is
  physical AI's standard body, MyoFullBody a candidate standard musculoskeletal model, MOTOR the solver between the two
  standards (owner, 2026-11-08). Each paragraph makes one point and leads into the next; no asides, setting lists or
  hedges in running text. The main body after the abstract is four sections, related work, method, results, discussion (owner, 2026-11-08: "it
  needs a related work, method, results, discussion, done"), about 3 pages of text in connected prose (related work
  carries the motivation and closes on what MOTOR adds; method has at most three run-in heads and no subsections; the
  discussion's last paragraph does the conclusion's work). With its figures and table the main body is **at most 4
  pages**: it ends at `\label{endofmain}` after a `\FloatBarrier`, and its page in `main.aux` is at most 4. Its
  register is the owner's published paper (arXiv:2607.17639): full academic prose, no project jargon.
  Method detail and full tables go to the appendices. MOTOR is the per-frame solver (owner, 2026-11-09, on #98: "report
  the fast per-frame solver as the result and make the whole-trial solve future work"): its results are the run of
  `evidence/labval-final-2026-11-07.txt`, and the whole-trial solve appears only as future work in the discussion, its
  numbers from `evidence/motor-labval-2026-11-09.txt`, labelled as the whole-trial solve's. OpenCap's numbers are its
  published ones; PBL is not rerun (owner, 2026-11-09: licence, its MeTRAbs models are non-commercial).
  Build with `--keep-intermediates`, then delete `main.aux`, `main.out` and `main.log`.
- `evidence/`: the shared run records that code reads as well as the papers, left at this path because paid rows'
  code names it: `cloud/` (the scrubbed cloud ledger, bill, capacity and cost tables; read by `tools/cloud/`,
  `tools/costs/`, `score/stages/` and `score/adapters/compute/`) and `compare-hub/` (the hub's prop-route table and
  sizes; read by `score/stages/complete/prepare/`).

## Rules for the draft

- **Every number is read from a file**, named in an HTML comment beside it. No number from memory. A new or rewritten source is public: a file in the repository (a run record goes into its paper's `evidence/`), a commit or an issue comment, never a personal path, a memory note or a private page. Gap: older comments still name run logs that are not published (git-ignored `tmp/` logs, handoffs, R&D progress logs); when a claim they carry is touched, its record moves into `evidence/` or the comment cites a commit or an issue comment.
- **The draft has the final paper's structure.** Settled content (problem, method) is written fully, tight, without hedging. Wherever final content will go (a figure, a results table, an evaluation), a gap box stands in its place: `::: gap` ... `:::`, starting `**Gap: <what>.**`, one or two sentences on what goes there and what it waits on. A gap is filled by replacing the box. `[text]{.todo}` (prints red) is only for a number still being checked, and does not reach a shared draft.
- **Short:** about 4 to 5 pages plus references while gaps are open; it grows as they fill (SCORE's paper; the MOTOR paper's main body is at most 4 pages, above). No filler: no disclaimers, no repeated hedges, related work only where it positions SCORE.
- **A change to the draft is rebuilt and committed in the same commit** as the PDF and LaTeX.
- **The walkable reference** (World Labs Marble in our runs) is one optional step of the method. Never
  compare it with another model or judge it on its own (World Labs ToS §2.8(d)); results are
  "route with" against "route without" the step. Credit "Generated using World Labs" wherever its
  output or anything derived from it is shown.
- **Disclose** what was automatic, what a coding agent wrote, what was a hand fix and what was a human pick.
- **Pictures:** our own outputs only, no real person's likeness, models whose licence allows commercial use.
- Top-level headings are `#`; a new heading becomes a new `content/` file by itself.
- **Write in the register of the owner's published paper** (arXiv:2607.17639; owner, 2026-11-08): full, connected
  academic sentences that argue and motivate; an introduction that builds from the broad field to the specific gap,
  its citations carrying the argument; methods that say why each choice is made; a discussion that draws out
  implications; a conclusion that restates the finding in context, says what the work removes or makes possible and
  ends on what it opens for the field. Not chat style: no staccato slogan lines, no stacked colon lists in running
  text, no run of short sentences; the chat style rules of `.claude/soul.md` do not apply to paper prose. Plain means
  clear and precise, not childish. Claims no stronger than the record; quote the owner only where the paper records
  what he said, and turn offhand remarks into proper wording.
