\begin{abstract}
Games, film, robotics and embodied AI all need 3D environments in which
every object is a physical body of its own, that can still be edited
after they are made, and that belong to whoever made them. World models
produce video that no simulator can open, and one-shot 3D generators
produce a single fused scene. SCORE is an open framework that
synthesises such environments the way film and game studios build them:
in OpenUSD, scene within scene, with layout, surfaces, physics,
lighting, motion, effects, sound and people each held in a layer of its
own. A creator locks the design from the top down. Open vision models,
procedural tools and coding agents produce every layer beneath it, and
each output must pass measured acceptance tests before the next is made.
The result is a single OpenUSD environment whose physics a simulator
reads as written, and every person in it also receives a musculoskeletal
body that follows its motion in simulation. This opens a route to the
training data that recorded measurement cannot supply at scale: movement
in complete scenes, with physics-derived ground truth down to the forces
of individual muscles. Code and paper:
https://github.com/Babon-Innovations-b-v/score (MIT licence).
\end{abstract}
