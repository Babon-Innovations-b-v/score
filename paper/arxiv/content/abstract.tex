\begin{abstract}
Games, films and robot training need 3D environments that are complete,
editable and owned by their makers; world models and one-shot generators
give frames no engine opens, or one fused scene without separate objects
or clear rights. Studios build environments in stages, from concept art
to review. SCORE keeps these stages and automates them with open vision
foundation models, procedural tools and coding agents, while the creator
chooses and reviews. Every stage is a node of one runner: its output is
keyed on its inputs and code, judged by gates that answer pass, fail or
unknown, and accepted only when nothing got worse, so a fault is fixed
in its own stage and only what follows it runs again; paid steps are
cleared against a budget read from the provider's bill. Every model
allows commercial use of its output, so the creator stays the author. We
describe the framework as built for its first environment, the game
environment of 2099 with 28 places, and the measured gates of its
surfaces, resting, light, motion, effects, sound and people. Code and
paper are available at https://github.com/Babon-Innovations-b-v/score
(MIT license).
\end{abstract}
