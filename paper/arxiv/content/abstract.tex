\begin{abstract}
World models and one-shot 3D generators can produce a convincing place
from a sentence or a picture, but not a world that a creator can build
on: a world model produces a stream of frames, and a 3D generator
usually produces one fused scene without separate objects or physical
properties. SCORE is a world generation framework that joins open vision
foundation models, procedural tools and coding agents into one route
from a creator's choices to a complete world that runs in the creator's
own engine. The creator chooses the look, the concept and the style of
each place, and stays the author of the world, holding full commercial
rights, because every model in the route allows commercial use of its
output. Between those choices, the route works offline, in batches on
rented cloud machines, through fixed stages. Every real-world object
becomes one model with real collision, mass and material; every surface
comes from a shared library of rule-based surfaces; and sound is
generated for every surface, room and object. Deterministic checks run
before every stage that costs money. The output of every stage is kept,
so the creator can trace a weak result to the stage that caused it,
rerun or fix that stage, and regenerate the stages after it while
keeping their own edits; review tools that the framework supplies show
each stage's output. We describe the framework and apply it to four
worlds of different kinds.
\end{abstract}
