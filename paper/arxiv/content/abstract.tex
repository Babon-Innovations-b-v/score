\begin{abstract}
Games, film, robotics and embodied AI need 3D environments in which
every object is a physical body of its own, that stay editable, and that
belong to whoever made them. World models produce video that no
simulator can open; one-shot 3D generators produce a single fused scene.
SCORE is an open framework that synthesises such environments the way
studios build them in OpenUSD, scene within scene and layer by layer,
every output passing measured tests before the next is made. Every
person in an environment also receives a musculoskeletal body that
follows its motion in simulation. This opens a route to training data
that recorded measurement cannot supply at scale: movement in complete
scenes, with physics-derived ground truth down to the forces of
individual muscles. Code: https://github.com/Babon-Innovations-b-v/score
(MIT).
\end{abstract}
