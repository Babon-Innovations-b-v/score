\section{Limitations, future work and conclusion}\label{limitations-future-work-and-conclusion}

One creator judges, and he has not yet accepted a run of environment 1. Closed models (Nano Banana Pro, Gemini 3.1 Pro) draw the concepts and close-ups and read and plan every scene; they may change or go. Much of the first run was lost with its disk, and the new design step, the finish route, the shapes-first loop and the code bodies have not yet carried a scene through a batch; people are not usable, light is live, objects are not interactive, and no game engine opens the result yet. Next: the remake's batch, the shapes-first loop on the ship, people, an engine adapter with physics and interaction, an edit layer the assembly carries, three more environments, open models, LEGO-Bench \citep{li2026lego}. In short, SCORE keeps, keys and judges every stage's output, so a fault is fixed where it happens; an accepted environment will show if it holds.
