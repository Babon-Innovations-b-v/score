\section{Limitations, future work and conclusion}\label{limitations-future-work-and-conclusion}

One creator judges, and he has not yet accepted a run. Closed models (Nano Banana Pro, Gemini 3.1 Pro) draw the concepts and close-ups and read and plan every scene; they may change or go. The design step, the finish route, the shapes-first loop and the code bodies have not yet carried a scene through a batch; the edit layer is a slot, objects are not interactive, and no game engine opens the result yet. Next: the remake's batch, an engine adapter with physics and interaction, an edit layer the assembly carries, three more environments, open models, LEGO-Bench \citep{li2026lego}. In short, SCORE builds an environment the way studios do, scene by scene and layer by layer, so a fault is fixed in its own layer; an accepted environment will show if it holds.
