\section{Limitations and outlook}\label{limitations-and-outlook}

Code builders are clean but need an agent to write one per kind of object, which does not scale as well as generation. A concept shows one side of a place, so built places come out sparser than their concepts; the concept-density check catches this but does not fix it, and the optional world step is meant to. Daily quotas on picture models, not compute cost, set the pace of a build. Next, the simulation world needs masses, friction and joints compiled into the scene for robots, and scales that are computed rather than modelled enter as open solvers wrapped as stages, starting with REBOUND for orbits \citep{rein2012rebound}.
