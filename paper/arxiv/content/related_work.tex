\section{Related work}\label{related-work}

SCORE sits between three lines of work. World models \citep{bruce2024genie, genie3} and 3D environment generators \citep{wang2025worldgen, hyworld2} generate an environment in one shot, as frames or as a fused asset, and recent work recovers separate objects from that asset afterwards \citep{hu2026worldact, niu2026worldsculpt}; SCORE instead makes every object separately from the start, and writes its body into the record. Procedural systems \citep{raistrick2023infinigen, raistrick2024indoors} keep scenes explicit through hand-written generators, and SCORE uses their shaders, through ProcFunc \citep{raistrick2026procfunc}, as the base of its surface library, while reading what to build from a creator's concept rather than from a fixed grammar. Coding agents that write scenes as programs \citep{hu2024scenecraft, li2026rcwm, guo2026worldclaw, yin2026autoue, he2026thinking, wang2026codeasworlds} are closest in spirit, but the scenes they write often float or come apart \citep{gao2026codebench3d}, they judge their own geometry unreliably \citep{li2026lego}, and on scenes rebuilt from video the strongest model scores 0.91 on static scenes but only 0.67 on dynamic ones \citep{shen2026codebench4d}. SCORE therefore gives such agents a narrow role, writing builders for the objects other routes fail on, and leaves judgement to measured gates.
