Picture-to-3D, parts, surface bakes, sound, checks and assembly are automatic. Coding agents (Claude Code \citep{claudecode}) write the plans, inventories and every code builder; in the hub's final round 86 of its 98 models were code. Hand steps remained: some generated objects turned to face the right way by eye, a few labels placed by hand before a rule replaced that. Concepts and verdicts are the creator's picks.
