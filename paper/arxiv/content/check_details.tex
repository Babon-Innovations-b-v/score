\section{Check details}\label{check-details}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Room checks} cast rays to find light leaking out through walls and roof, find pieces in front of a real opening and roof and wall fittings more than 3 cm off their surface or turned more than 5 degrees from it, and count pieces, triangles and lamps against the room's budget.
\item
  \textbf{Door check:} every door must stand in a wall or partition that fully separates its two sides, tested by walking a grid from one side to the other.
\item
  \textbf{Parts check:} in the code build, every part shown in the close-up must be at least 10 percent visible from the front, and no label may cover a part.
\item
  \textbf{Model check:} every model must have walls at least 3 mm thick; whether a model is closed is recorded on each model and reported, not used to refuse it. A generated box fails if one of its sides tilts more than 2 degrees or is warped. Making a generated model solid stops if its bounding box grows more than 3 percent.
\item
  \textbf{Palette sweep:} every model's colours are compared with the place's library palette.
\item
  \textbf{Made-only check:} in the engine, any visible mesh that the framework did not make fails. A made-only finding can never be waived.
\item
  \textbf{Close-up shape check:} the picture needs a plain border all round, one object filling a sensible share of the frame, and an outline within a set proportion of the inventory row's box. The judge's seven questions (among them one object, a clean background, the whole object, the same object, no parts missing or added, the right proportions) each pass by a majority of three answers sampled with different seeds. A close-up passes only when both halves pass.
\item
  \textbf{Settling and resting check:} an object that would turn more than 15 degrees or drift more than 30 cm when dropped keeps its laid pose and is marked as not resting; small debris, with no side over 1.4 m, may turn freely and drift up to 1 m. Hung objects are exempt by their inventory anchor, and fixed objects are not judged for tipping.
\item
  \textbf{People checks:} a person's look rides SOMA-X's body fitted just inside it. The shell is a proxy gate: it passes when 99\% of a look's soft points lie within 5 cm of the fitted body, with the hard parts (boots, helmets, tools), the hair and the hands riding one joint each and belts worn as cloth, and the per-clip checks (body through cloth, cloth through cloth, tucks) decide which clips a look may use. The A-pose outline is judged against the finished Pixal3D take the build starts from; the take's own loss against the raw model is recorded on its own.
\item
  \textbf{Completion gate:} the requirements given in Section 3.1, each with its result tied to a fingerprint of the stage's files and inputs; a result made on another stage counts as unknown. The placeholder check judges every mesh a code builder draws, and only the shell builders pass unnamed.
\end{itemize}
