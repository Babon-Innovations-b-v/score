\section{Check details}\label{check-details}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Room checks} cast rays to find light leaking out through walls and roof, find pieces in front of a real opening and roof and wall fittings more than 3 cm off their surface or turned more than 5 degrees from it, and count pieces, triangles and lamps against the room's budget.
\item
  \textbf{Door check:} every door must stand in a wall or partition that fully separates its two sides, tested by walking a grid from one side to the other.
\item
  \textbf{Model check:} every model must have walls at least 3 mm thick. A generated box fails if one of its sides tilts more than 2 degrees or is warped. Making a generated model solid stops if its bounding box grows more than 3 percent.
\item
  \textbf{Likeness gate:} a made model's outline from eight sides must overlap its close-up's by at least 0.50, a least set on the hub's twelve accepted generated models with a margin of 0.05, and its DINOv2 \citep{oquab2023dinov2} similarity must reach a least set the same way; without that calibration the gate answers unknown.
\item
  \textbf{Finish maps:} a close-up's map passes when its outline overlaps the close-up's by at least 0.85 and it uses every listed finish; a part whose second finish holds 20 percent of its voted area mixes finishes and fails.
\item
  \textbf{Bake check:} each baked part within ΔE 20 (CIE76) of its surface's colour, worn and dirtied as far as its place's wear and dirt, and within ΔE 32 of its close-up's; lightness order fails when the close-up shows one surface more than 10 L* lighter and the bake makes it more than 3 L* darker; one look fails when no two near-neutral surfaces bake far enough apart for a viewer to tell at the place's view distance, while the close-up shows them more than ΔE 24 apart. What a viewer tells is measured, not set: Stone, Szafir and Setlur measured the CIELAB difference half of their viewers see between two colours s degrees wide, per axis ND(50, s) = C + K/s, with (C, K) = (5.08, 0.75) for L*, (5.34, 1.54) for a* and (5.35, 2.87) for b* on sizes 1/3 to 6 degrees \citep{stone2014size}; two surfaces are told apart when their difference, each axis over its ND, is 1 or more. A surface's size is its model's middle size times the root of its area share, seen from the place's nearest view. This replaced a fixed ΔE 12, which failed the wreck's access cover on a 9.3 L* step between its steel and its scorched white that its owner sees (11.3 degrees from the nearest view; half of viewers see 5.2 L* at that size); a quadrant of a standing model's base more than 3 cm off the floor fails. Glass, screens, lamps and print are judged by their own colour and left out of the order.
\item
  \textbf{Settling and resting check:} an object that would turn more than 15 degrees or drift more than 30 cm when dropped keeps its laid pose and is marked as not resting; small debris, with no side over 1.4 m, may turn freely and drift up to 1 m. Hung objects are exempt by their inventory anchor, and fixed objects are not judged for tipping. The triage fails a foot off its support and an up axis leaning more than 3 degrees.
\item
  \textbf{Light:} the place's median brightness within 1.5 times its concept's and every view within 2.5 times; a lamp is blown when over 20 percent of the pixels where it gives at least half the light are white, and unlit when it gives less than 0.001 percent of every view's light.
\item
  \textbf{Motion:} no moving thing within 3 cm of a still thing or another moving thing, read every 0.25 s; rounds join within 1 mm; speeds within each kind's cited range.
\item
  \textbf{Sound:} CLAP score against the brief at least 0.30, the tenth percentile of 55 of the environment's picks; a take no more than 0.5 s longer than its brief.
\item
  \textbf{People:} the shell gate passes a look when 99 percent of its soft points lie within 5 cm of the fitted body; the worst point and its joint are recorded. A clip is usable when no frame tucks more than 5 mm, no hard part stretches past 1.2 times its bind length, cloth crosses cloth no more than in the earlier body's same clip, and no part's edges pass through cloth deeper than 3 mm more often than at the bind. A far-only look is judged on its joins, likeness and the far body's triangles (at most 1,220); the near outfit's triangles and shell offset are recorded, not gating. The A-pose outline is judged against the finished Pixal3D take the build starts from; the take's own loss against the raw model is recorded apart. A crowd copy stands only where it fills under a tenth of every view's height.
\item
  \textbf{Worse than accepted:} a measure counts as worse only when it moves the wrong way by at least its tolerance, the smallest change a viewer could see: one more fault for a count; 0.02 of full scale for lightness on 0 to 1 (a halo, fill, beam or haze wash), and 5 of 255 levels for a colour gap, the step a viewer notices between two pictures; 0.05 for a brightness ratio; one percent of the picture for a share of it; a twentieth of the Earth's lit disc; 5 mm for a length. A score no viewer reads directly, such as a likeness score, is worse by any move. Tolerances decide only whether an output waits for the creator; a check that fails still fails.
\item
  \textbf{The creator's accept carried forward:} when the creator accepts a worse output, that accept records what was allowed, each measure's value before and after. A later output that passes every gate and is no worse than the output taken, within the tolerances, is accepted by the runner with that accept named; a move beyond it still waits for the creator.
\item
  \textbf{What the creator acts on:} the creator rejects, accepts or waives only an output already seen on its review page. This is the creator's rule; the commands do not yet check that the page was opened.
\item
  \textbf{People seen by the cameras (experimental):} a clip's crossings of body through cloth are counted only where a camera that will show the person can see them: the place's views at the person's cast spot, and a close front and three-quarter view. Crossings no camera sees are recorded, not counted against the clip. So far this is a measure on the stored bodies (Section 3.5); the per-clip gate does not use it yet.
\item
  \textbf{Completion gate:} the requirements given in Section 3.6, each with its result tied to a fingerprint of the stage's files and inputs; a result made on another stage counts as unknown. The placeholder check judges every mesh a code builder draws, and only the shell builders pass unnamed.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{People results in detail (experimental, Section 3.5).} The body gates are joins, outline, likeness, triangles and shell. Nev's and Sefa's boot tuck bands lie 9 to 11 mm outside the trouser leg at the bind pose, in every clip before any motion. Scored on drawn surfaces only, no clip of any look is usable: skin seams open and settled cloth crosses itself beyond the bind. Bound as one closed mesh, the player's limbs sink into chest and hips, 87 to 563 crossing points visible from the places' cameras and close views per clip, the armpit surface stretching between arm and chest. Where hair or cloth meets the head or hand skin the two sides are weighted differently and open as the joints move (cloth seams never open); openings over 10 cm on three looks are not yet confirmed by a render. Binding the take as one closed mesh removes the openings. Pushing points out of the look's own volume, limiting the offset from SOMA-X at the armpits and inner thighs, fold-centred weights and dual-quaternion skinning each left the visible crossings the same or higher. A close-up drawn in a wider A-pose (arms 45 degrees below level, feet 35 cm apart) gives a take with fewer crossings at the bind (the player's 678 against 772) but more in motion, as the clips bring the arms further down: 10,610 visible crossings against 4,078 over the player's 19 clips, and no clip clean in either pose. The clips' joint angles stay within the normal human ranges of the American Academy of Orthopaedic Surgeons, except hip extension of 24 and 27 degrees (limit 20) in two clips.

\textbf{Decisions recorded in these checks.} Three people's gates are decisions of the captain, the agent session that runs environment 1, taken on 12 and 13 October 2026 under the creator's rules and open to the creator's reversal. (1) The shell gate counts 99 percent of the soft points rather than every point, with the worst point recorded: the points beyond it were loose cloth edges (cuffs, hems, caps), which no part label names, so they could not be given a joint of their own by label. (2) A look seen only far away is judged on what is drawn far away, not on the near outfit it was made from. (3) The A-pose outline is judged against the finished take, not the raw model, so the take's own loss is not charged to the build. Earlier, the full-body cut (which looks get bodies in full and which only far ones) was set by the people's share of the budget.
