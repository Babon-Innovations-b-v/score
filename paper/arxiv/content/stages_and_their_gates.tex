\section{Stages and their gates}\label{stages-and-their-gates}

\subsection{Surfaces: the finish route}\label{surfaces-the-finish-route}

Everything a scene shows comes from the creator's library, score\_lib, or is a written exception (the creator's rule of 18 October 2026). Models give shape only: a picture-made model keeps its geometry and its finish is replaced. Every part takes one surface from a shared library of rule-based surfaces, written as ProcFunc functions \citep{raistrick2026procfunc} on Infinigen's shaders \citep{raistrick2023infinigen}, coloured from the scene's palette and worn only where wear has a cause. The library starts empty for the remake: every surface and model of the earlier runs was retired on 21 October 2026, so the batch makes them fresh. Each scene's palette is coined from its locked concept, one Gemini 3.1 Pro batch answer gives its surfaces as settings of the recipes, and an entry is reused only when made under today's route.

The finish route gives each model its finishes (the creator's choice of 20 October 2026). Free renders cover every face of the raw model, each view split into colour regions; one Gemini 3.1 Pro answer a model lists its finishes, each matched to a library surface, and labels each region, and the labels vote across views into one finish a face, a face no label reaches or whose views disagree being a gap. Each part is painted from its library surface and baked, and the bake is checked against the close-up: a finish's surface must lie within ΔE 32 of its colour there, a bake fails when it darkens a finish the close-up shows lighter or turns all surfaces into one grey, and a foot over 3 cm off the floor fails. A free library check traces every material, model, light, sound and character of a scene's accepted assembly to a library entry or a written exception.

\subsection{Resting}\label{resting}

Loose objects are dropped onto the made ground in a physics simulation; one that would turn more than 15 degrees or drift more than 30 cm is a resting fault of the layout and keeps its laid pose. The settle stage lays where each object comes to rest as a layer (held objects snapped onto their holders, a stack onto one support, an object that would rest worse kept as laid), gated on \texttt{rested} and \texttt{resting} (no fault that appears with the layer and not without it). A physics layer writes the scene's physics in OpenUSD, and a trial on Bullet drops the loose rows' objects where they stand, under the gravity of the body the scene stands on (\texttt{reacted}, \texttt{at\ rest}, \texttt{energy}, \texttt{hinge}).

\subsection{Light}\label{light}

The sky is worked out from the EC's physics, never drawn: the light layer writes what the scene's top site sees overhead at its scene time, each body at its true direction, size and phase (\texttt{sky\ bodies}), and a light under the horizon lights nothing. The lamps are the inventory's glowing rows, and their strengths are measured. One paid render per scene gives every lamp its own Cycles light group on the scene's views; because light adds up, the look at any strengths is mixed from these passes without rendering again, and a free sweep chooses the scale that brings the scene's brightness within 1.5 times its concept's, halving any lamp that turns what it lights white.

\subsection{Motion, effects and sound}\label{motion-effects-and-sound}

\textbf{Motion.} Every object of a moving row moves by its kind's rule (a door swings and shuts, a crane slews, a fan spins) at the middle of its kind's speed range cited from real machines, gated on \texttt{clear} (no moving thing within 3 cm of a still thing or another moving thing, read every quarter second), \texttt{speeds}, \texttt{loops}, \texttt{grounded}, \texttt{covered} and \texttt{designed}. \textbf{Effects:} an emitting row's objects carry their kind's emitters, measured against real footage, gated on \texttt{attached}, \texttt{footage}, \texttt{light}, \texttt{outside} (no open-sky particle inside a room) and \texttt{designed}. \textbf{Sound:} a scene's sources come from its design (a bed from how the scene is heard, its sounding rows, its people's voices and its moving things), and each gets a library file or one MOSS-SoundEffect \citep{moss2026} take of its brief, gated on \texttt{sources}, \texttt{bed}, \texttt{events}, \texttt{voices}, \texttt{level}, \texttt{likeness} (CLAP \citep{wu2022clap} score at least 0.30; a take under it is a gap) and \texttt{no\ freesound}.

\subsection{People}\label{people}

People are models like any other. The looks come from the person rows of the locked designs, each person from its own SAM 3 box on the concept and a crowd as one far look; the shared loop draws its close-up (one person, front on, in the rig's A-pose), Pixal3D builds it and the finish route paints it. The rigged body takes each part's library surface from that paint, its face and hair included, and the library surfaces are baked onto the body itself as onto any model and checked the same way; no picture's finish reaches it. SOMA-X's body, read from the close-up by SAM 3D Body \citep{yang2026sam3dbody}, is fitted under it as its rig, and Kimodo's clips \citep{rempe2026kimodo}, from sentences, move it. The \textbf{shell gate} passes a look when 99 percent of its soft points lie within 5 cm of the body, its worst point recorded; each clip gets its own verdict, and casts use only passing clips. \textbf{Casts:} each person stands on a person spot of its scene's plan, gated on \texttt{counted}, \texttt{standing}, \texttt{clear} and \texttt{made}.

\subsection{Completion}\label{completion}

A scene is complete when its gate passes on its final assembly: every row made or dropped with a reason, nothing unmade, no placeholder or resting fault, the creator's picks shown and every layer and shown scene present. A fault the creator decides to ship with is an accepted gap, written with who decided, when, why and its measured faults; the completion gate lists every gap by name and is never loosened, and a scene whose accepted gaps are its only fail is complete apart from accepted gaps. A hook stops an agent claiming a scene is done while its gate fails: agents judging their own scenes agree with a measured geometry score only 45.8 percent of the time \citep{li2026lego}.

\subsection{The film}\label{the-film}

The film is a design step too: the creator locks a film script in the cockpit (by default the whole environment, body by body), and a scene's films are made from locked scripts only, once it is complete or complete apart from accepted gaps.

\subsection{Prices and ledgers}\label{prices-and-ledgers}

Every paid stage is priced from a unit cost measured on the ledgers, times the units its forecast rule counts, times the bill's share over the ledger; a stage with no measured run has no price, and its estimate is shown apart as a guess. Today 15 of the table's 29 paid kinds of row have a measured unit price, four of them from two benchmark batches of 21 October 2026 that the creator approved, one node a kind. Machines and Google calls write ledger lines with their node's clearance, and the month's spend is the higher of ledgers and bill, which caught misses: EUR 1,554 billed against EUR 1,387 in the cloud ledger on 12 October 2026. A paid node runs only when every gate on its inputs passed, the month's books are known and its batch's stop has room for its forecast.
