\section{Limitations and conclusion}\label{limitations-and-conclusion}

A single creator judges the results, and he has not yet accepted a run. Closed models (Nano Banana Pro, Gemini 3.1 Pro) draw the concepts and close-ups and read and plan every scene, and may change or be withdrawn. The design step, the layers derived from row kinds, the finish route, the shapes-first loop and the scene tree have not yet carried a scene through a batch. The edit layer is so far only a reserved slot. The settle, the effects and the sim body do not yet run in Newton as stages, and no game engine or robot simulator has yet opened the result. Next come environment 1's batch with its four demonstrations, an engine adapter, the edit layer carried across assemblies, further environments, open models replacing the closed ones, and the data loop, in which residual-filtered generated movement trains muscle-driven controllers whose value a comparison with motion capture has yet to show. SCORE synthesises an environment as studios build one, scene by scene and layer by layer, in a form a simulator steps as written, and an accepted environment will show whether the approach holds.
