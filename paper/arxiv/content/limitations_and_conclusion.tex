\section{Limitations and conclusion}\label{limitations-and-conclusion}

One creator judges, and he has not yet accepted a run. Closed models (Nano Banana Pro, Gemini 3.1 Pro) draw the concepts and close-ups and read and plan every scene; they may change or go. The design step, the layers from row kinds, the finish route, the shapes-first loop and the scene tree have not yet carried a scene through a batch; the edit layer is a slot, the settle, the effects and the sim body are not yet on Newton, and no game engine or robot simulator has opened the result yet. Next: environment 1's batch and its four demonstrations, an engine adapter, the edit layer carried across assemblies, more environments, open models in place of the closed ones. In short, SCORE synthesizes an environment the way studios build one, scene by scene and layer by layer, in a form a simulator steps as written; an accepted environment will show whether it holds.
