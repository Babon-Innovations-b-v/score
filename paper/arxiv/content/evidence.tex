Four worlds are planned; world 1's 18 places are judged once their layers stand accepted.

Takes in a wider A-pose crossed less at the bind and more in motion (10,610 visible crossings on the player's clips against 4,078), with no clip clean in either pose. People therefore ship as named gaps: the characters layer lays the people whose bodies stand accepted, and the completion gate fails every missing one by name.
