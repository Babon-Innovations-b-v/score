\section{Limitations and future work}\label{limitations-and-future-work}

Every verdict is one creator's; he accepted the hub before the runner. Closed models (Nano Banana Pro, Gemini 3.1 Pro) draw concepts, close-ups and finish maps and can change price or be retired. Hard belts failed deep bends. Light is live, not baked, measured on earlier layouts; objects are not interactive. Next: three more worlds, an engine adapter, interactable objects, baked per-lamp light, open close-up and map models, a library of locked parts, LEGO-Bench \citep{li2026lego} and a style measure.
