\section{Evidence: environment 1}\label{evidence-environment-1}

Environment 1 serves as the evidence. The creator did not accept its first run, and its store of outputs was lost on 19 October 2026, so this draft shows none of its outputs, and each demonstration below will be produced by its new batch. Figure 1 is the first.

\begin{center}\fbox{\begin{minipage}{0.94\linewidth}\setlength{\parskip}{4pt}\small

\textbf{Gap: Demonstration 2, physics that holds.} Environment 1's scenes stepped on Newton under their own body's gravity: loose objects come to rest, doors swing on their hinges, with the physics trial's gates per scene and Bullet run on the same scenes as the baseline. It waits on environment 1's batch and on Newton taking over the settle.

\end{minipage}}\end{center}

\begin{center}\fbox{\begin{minipage}{0.94\linewidth}\setlength{\parskip}{4pt}\small

\textbf{Gap: Demonstration 3, one environment, many uses.} The same OpenUSD environment opened unchanged in a game engine, in a robot simulator and in a film render, with what each reads of its layers and what it drops. It waits on environment 1's batch and on the engine adapter.

\end{minipage}}\end{center}

\begin{center}\fbox{\begin{minipage}{0.94\linewidth}\setlength{\parskip}{4pt}\small

\textbf{Gap: Demonstration 4, an edit that stays cheap.} The creator changes one thing in a locked scene, and only the nodes whose keys it moves run again; the nodes rerun, time and euros against a full rebuild. It waits on environment 1's batch and on the edit layer being carried from one assembly to the next.

\end{minipage}}\end{center}

\begin{center}\fbox{\begin{minipage}{0.94\linewidth}\setlength{\parskip}{4pt}\small

\textbf{Gap: Table 1.} Per scene of environment 1: inventory rows, models generated and code-built, layers, gaps before and after the batch, time, euros and the creator's verdict. It waits on environment 1's batch and the creator's review.

\end{minipage}}\end{center}

\textbf{Disclosure.} The creator's locks, picks, accepts and waivers are his own actions in the cockpit, and everything between them is automatic. Coding agents wrote most of the framework's code under the author's direction, and 2,413 of its 2,436 commits list a coding agent as co-author. Manual fixes to a scene are recorded as the creator's edits.
