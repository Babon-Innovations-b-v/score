\section{Discussion}\label{discussion}

The design rests on one observation, that an environment generated step by step, with every step held to a measured gate and keyed on its inputs, can carry its physics and its authorship in the record itself. The consequences of this are concrete, although until environment 1's batch has run they are properties of the design and not yet measured results:

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Faults have an address.} Because each layer is made by its own node and judged by its own gates, a fault is found at the step that caused it and not in a finished scene, and fixing it moves only the keys downstream of that step. Demonstration 4 is to measure what this saves.
\item
  \textbf{One record for many readers.} The physics is written in OpenUSD's physics schema and not in one engine's format, so any reader of that schema obtains the same bodies, joints and gravity. Demonstration 3 is to test this in a game engine, a robot simulator and a film renderer.
\item
  \textbf{The creator stays the author.} Every model allows commercial use of its output, every finish traces to the creator's library, and the decisions that shape the result (locks, accepts, rejects and waivers) are his own, recorded with their reasons.
\item
  \textbf{Agents are not their own judges.} A coding agent writes code builders and connects tools, but it never decides that its own work is done; that decision belongs to measured gates and, above them, to the creator.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Limitations}\label{limitations}

\textbf{One creator and no accepted run.} A single creator judges every environment, and he has not yet accepted a run; the method has therefore not yet been shown to produce an environment its maker would ship.

\textbf{Closed models.} Nano Banana Pro and Gemini 3.1 Pro draw the concepts and close-ups and read and plan every scene. They are closed and may change or be withdrawn, and replacing them with open models remains to be done.

\textbf{Unexercised stages.} The design step, the layers from row kinds, the finish route, the shapes-first loop and the scene tree have not yet carried a scene through a batch. The edit layer is still a slot, the settle, the effects and the sim body are not yet on Newton, and no game engine or robot simulator has yet opened the result.
