FONTLOG for the Noto Sans SC files in this folder

These are modified versions of Noto Sans SC (Google, from Adobe's Source Han Sans), used under the
SIL Open Font License 1.1 in OFL.txt beside them. The family name is kept: the licence reserves
only the name "Source", which none of these files carries.

NotoSansSC-Regular.otf (2026-09-27): Noto Sans SC Regular, cut to the GB2312 characters plus
ASCII and the CJK punctuation, its line box set to its ideographs' own 880/-120, as the fallback
of every Barlow font in the interface.

NotoSansSC-Bold.ttf and NotoSansSC-Black.ttf (2026-10-04): the variable Noto Sans SC from the
Google Fonts repository (ofl/notosanssc/NotoSansSC[wght].ttf, sha256
a3041811a78c361b1de50f953c805e0244951c21c5bd412f7232ef0d899af0da), instanced at weight 700 and 900
with fontTools.varLib.instancer (names updated), then cut with fontTools.subset to the 7,445
GB2312 characters, ASCII, the CJK symbols and punctuation (U+3000 to U+303F), the full-width forms
(U+FF00 to U+FFEF) and · — … ‘ ’ “ ” × №, every layout feature and name kept, hinting dropped.
They draw the Mandarin signs on the Moon base and on Earth (game/art/signs/): Bold on plates,
panels and frames, Black on banners.
