Each prop sits beside the picture it was built from, drawn in ink over its own texture. Compare the two: what the picture shows clearly is what came through. Drag any prop to turn it.

%%PROPS%%

Reading these

  • The model rebuilds silhouette and big solid form. If the prop is wrong, look at the picture first: a surface it hides, or shows nearly edge-on, is a surface the mesh had to guess.
  • Small raised detail comes back as soft bulges. Latches, brackets, handles and labels belong in ink lines and flat colour, not in the mesh.
  • More triangles and more sampling steps do not fix a bowed prop. Rewrite the sentence so the object is thick solid panels, and take the picture again.
