# score/stages/light/

The light stages (paper-vs-world-1 items 9 and 17), their own runner (`python -m score.stages.light run|status|why
<node>`): `lightkit` (world, paid: the square's proscenium backdrop and round-lamp floodlight towers, baked with the
library's surfaces), `sweep@<place>` (one record number tried at several strengths) and `light@<place>` (the place
exported again and rendered on the round-three demo's own views, frames 000 and 128, on rented cards).

- **Every fault is measured before and after on the same views** and against the place's concept (`measure.py`):
  floodlight heads (halo, fill, beam, veil) against K04, the Earth's smooth share against C4, Mars's sky colour
  against K05. A pass rule is set before any after-picture is made; a rule changed later says why in its judge.
- **Only heads facing the eye are judged** (`FACING_LEAST`); a head with no shaft light of its own is still measured.
- **The launch's pad lamps fail the heads gate** (halo about 0.03 against 0.08, seen from 200 m), so they stay as
  main has them and `light@launch` fails on them alone; the square's towers it shows pass there.
- Sweeps write their chosen strength into the scene record by hand from their output; the light nodes read the record.
