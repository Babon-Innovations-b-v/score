# score/stages/sim_body/inside/tuning/_adjoint/

Level 1's Jacobian by implicit differentiation at the inner optimum (#70, card plan step 6b). Numpy only in `__init__.py`, callables in:
the inner gradient g(z, theta) over z = (every frame's pose, then the body's constants) and the residual rows R(z, theta).

- dR/dtheta = R_theta - R_z H^-1 dg/dtheta. H is the FULL Hessian by central differences of g, never Gauss-Newton
  (the card study measured Gauss-Newton alone 22.4 % off, the full one 0.19 % from a re-solve).
- H is read block-arrow: one pose coordinate of every frame stepped together, then each constant alone
  (2 x (pose size + constants) gradients, not 2 x inner size); solved by the Schur step over the constants.
- `rowed_jacobian` (2026-10-30) is `implicit_jacobian` with R_z and R_theta given (taped rows) in place of central
  differences of the residuals, the shared H^-1 dg/dtheta in `inner_motion`; on the toy, hand-written rows equal
  differences of the re-solved fit. `chained_jacobian` still differences its residuals (not yet switched to rows).
- `adjoint_rows` (2026-10-30, speed): `chained_rows` in reverse mode, one solve per outer ROW not per column:
  lambda = H3^-T R_q^T, D = (R_theta | R_c) - lambda^T g3_(q,theta c), mu = H2^-T [0; D_c^T], rows D_theta - mu^T
  g2_(z,theta); each H read once (`adjoint_solve`) for every right-hand side. `transposed_mixed` takes w^T dg/dtheta
  from a Body's optional `products(inner, outer, weights)` (meant: two taped outer gradients a row, d/dtheta of g . w
  by symmetry), else `mixed` once. On the toy it equals `chained_rows` and the re-solves; level 3's `products` is built (toy only), level 2 still falls back to `mixed` in `_check_columns`. `_level2.sited_products` (`level2_body(..., sited=(columns, step))`, 2026-10-30) is level 2's: the costs' part 2 J^T dr/dc (each row linear in its cost) through `goal_weights`, the sites' part differenced over every moved site row; on `_toys.CostedSites` equal to w^T `mixed` to 1.6e-6 (red 0.24 without the 2). Not wired: heavy_kimodo moves 306 site rows (240 free, 66 fixed), so it would take 612 gradient calls against `mixed`'s 526; it pays only with the free marks' rows off H. `sited=(columns, step, arrow)` (2026-10-30) takes each free mark's site row whose constant is not held off `level2_arrow`'s H (its constant's column less the prior's cost squared on its diagonal), no gradient call; on `_toys.FreeSites` equal to w^T `mixed` under 1e-4 of its largest (red 0.11 without the diagonal). Not yet wired into `_check_columns`.
